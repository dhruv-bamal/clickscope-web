Square icon-only control for copy, QR, kebab, and close actions. The `label` prop is mandatory — it becomes the accessible name.

```jsx
<IconButton icon="copy" label="Copy link" />
<IconButton icon="ellipsis-vertical" label="Row actions" />
<IconButton icon="x" label="Close" variant="secondary" />
```

`size="lg"` (44px) for touch-primary contexts.
